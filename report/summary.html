When is a coach's challenge worth it?

Teams won 63% of 4,407 regulation challenges. The biggest factor is what's being challenged: out-of-bounds and possession calls were overturned 88% of the time, fouls 48%, and goaltending only 10%. The odds of success fall 53% in the last two minutes of the 4th. Score margin made no measurable difference.

Method & caveats

  • Each challenge is the “Instant Replay – Coach Challenge” event in NBA.com play-by-play. The outcome is Overturn (success) vs. Support / Stands. The challenging team comes from the timeout called at that moment.
  • Timeouts left come from the league's running timeout tally before the challenge. Score margin is from the challenging team's side.
  • Call-type leak: when a foul is overturned, NBA.com usually deletes it from the log. 719 challenges (16%) had no call logged at that moment, and 88% of them succeeded. These are treated as fouls. That keeps the foul family unbiased, but it also means foul subtypes (shooting vs. loose-ball, etc.) can't be separated reliably. Overturned out-of-bounds calls show up as the corrected possession, so they are pooled into one “out of bounds / possession” family.
  • This is selection-biased: it only covers calls coaches chose to challenge. It doesn't tell you how often an unchallenged call would have been overturned.
  • Fit: AUC 0.75, Brier 0.187 vs. 0.232 for always guessing the base rate (5-fold CV). The situation explains only part of the outcome. Most of it is what the coach saw on the replay.
